# Paired benchmark — Audio Agent V1
10 public Italian example recordings (two speaker IDs), five conditions each:
clean, white noise 10 dB, fan-like synthetic noise 5 dB, mains hum 10 dB,
attenuated 18 dB. Compare identical inputs with and without agent, fixed base
Whisper, no prompt or previous-text conditioning. WER, CER and critical-word
diagnostics. Runtime samples are not committed; source advertises a commercial
license, check terms before any further use or distribution. Synthetic noise is
not equivalent to real room recordings, and two speakers cannot establish
population-level performance. Do not activate agent by default based on this
small benchmark. No physical microphone changes. Next: independent evaluation
with consented real recordings and separate validation set, multi-engine A/B.
